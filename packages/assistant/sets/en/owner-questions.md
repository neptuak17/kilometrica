# The owner's own questions (A2, D77, D80)

Written by the owner on 2026-10-23, in their own words, before seeing any drafted questions. They seed the English
question sets; each will get what a good answer does. Some are meant to be declined (D77, D80).

Route-network routes (EV8, EV15) and riders' own routes ask much the same; for riders' own routes it is more often
about whether the route is feasible on sparse information. One set of questions and answers serves both.

## EV8 and EV15: route-network routes in developed settings

### Dream

1. I have 10 days in September to ride somewhere on EuroVelo 8. I like beaches and cultural experiences. I am reasonably fit, but want a relaxed experience. Can you suggest a couple of options?
2. Can you build a route that includes spending a few days in Barcelona somewhere in the middle of a 7 day trip?
3. Suggest a route that is flat and easy for myself and my family to ride that we can cover at a relaxed pace in 5 days. We want sunny weather and beaches and hotels at every stop.
4. When would be a better time of the year to ride this route?
5. I would like to stick to Italy for my trip, is there a route that fits?
6. Can I avoid Greece altogether?
7. Are there any routes without ferries?
8. There are too many hills on this route, can you suggest another?
9. This route isn't challenging enough, suggest other routes or way to increase the challenge?
10. Am I fit enough to do this route?
11. Will it rain while I am on this route?
12. Will this route be too hot for me?
13. What should I pack for this route?
14. Will I be safe on this route?

### Plan

1. Can you add a rest day after day 5 somewhere with a hotel and restaurants?
2. Is there any way to make day 7 easier?
3. I don't want to stop at x, can I stop at y instead?
4. Could I take a day off the trip and still get it done?
5. Why is day 5 so long?
6. Day 7 is too short - I'd like to go further that day
7. I don't like the accommodation options for day 6, is there somewhere better to stay?
8. How does May compare to September for this route?
9. How hot/rainy/windy/smoky is July on this route?
10. Is Barcelona safe to stay in?
11. Is the hotel in Avignon nice?

## Riders' own routes: often more remote and less developed, with less information

### Dream

1. Is this route doable in the time I have and for the level I am at?
2. How many days should this route take at my level?
3. When is best to do this route to avoid cold/heat/rain/smoke?
4. I prefer camping every night, can I do that on this route?
5. I prefer staying in hotels every night, can I do that on this route?
6. Is this route safe to do?

### Plan

1. I want to overnight in x, y and z, will it work?
2. Are there any resupply/water refill points between x and y?
3. What is the road surface like on day 5?
4. I don't want to ride at night - can I finish my days by 3pm so I can set up camp?
5. Are there any transportation options from the end of my route?
6. Is there space available at the campground on day 4?
7. How big is the climb on day 5?
8. Where can I get food/water on day 3?
